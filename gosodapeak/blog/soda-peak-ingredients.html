Soda Peak Ingredients:
A Science-Backed Breakdown of All 6 Compounds

The Soda Peak Formula at a Glance

Ingredient #1: Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

Primary roles: Stress reduction, cortisol management, testosterone support

Ashwagandha is arguably the most well-studied adaptogen in men's wellness. Its active compounds — withanolides — interact with the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, helping your body regulate its stress response more efficiently.

Multiple randomized controlled trials have examined Ashwagandha in men under physical and psychological stress. A frequently cited study published in the Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition found that men taking Ashwagandha extract for 8 weeks showed significant improvements in muscle recovery, strength, and reported reductions in exercise-induced muscle damage compared to placebo.

Critically for male performance: cortisol and testosterone have an inverse relationship. When cortisol stays chronically elevated — due to work stress, poor sleep, or overtraining — testosterone production typically declines. Ashwagandha's primary mechanism for supporting male vitality appears to operate through this cortisol-reduction pathway.

What the NIH says: The NIH has highlighted Ashwagandha research as promising for stress-related outcomes, noting that human evidence supports its adaptogenic effects, though optimal doses are still being studied.

Ingredient #2: Maca Root (Lepidium meyenii)

Primary roles: Libido enhancement, energy, adaptogenic support

Maca is a cruciferous vegetable native to the high Andes of Peru, where it has been cultivated for over 3,000 years primarily for its effects on male fertility and vitality. What makes Maca scientifically interesting is that its libido-supporting effects appear to operate independently of testosterone — meaning it can support male desire and energy through non-hormonal pathways, making it potentially effective for men at any age.

Notably, the NCCIH reports that small human studies suggest Maca may improve sexual dysfunction and desire in men. A 2010 systematic review identified four placebo-controlled trials showing Maca's positive effects on sexual dysfunction and sexual desire, though the authors acknowledged the need for larger trials.

Maca is also classified as an adaptogen, meaning it helps the body build resilience to physical and environmental stressors — complementing Ashwagandha's stress-management role in the formula.

Ingredient #3: Muira Puama (Ptychopetalum olacoides)

Primary roles: Libido, nerve health, circulation

Sometimes referred to as "potency wood" in traditional South American herbalism, Muira Puama is a small tree native to the Brazilian Amazon. Its bark has been used for centuries in traditional medicine for male sexual health and nervous system support.

Modern research on Muira Puama is earlier stage than Ashwagandha or Maca, but preliminary work suggests it may influence the central nervous system's role in sexual arousal, and support healthy blood flow — a dual mechanism that makes it a valuable component of male performance formulas.

In a Brazilian clinical observation involving men reporting low libido, Muira Puama extract showed notable improvements in desire and sexual function, though this study was uncontrolled. More rigorous research is ongoing.

Ingredient #4: Catuaba Bark (Trichilia catigua)

Primary roles: Circulation, central nervous system support, traditional male tonic

Catuaba is another Amazonian plant with deep roots in Brazilian traditional medicine, where it's been classified as a male aphrodisiac and nervous system tonic for generations. Its active compounds include alkaloids (catuabine A, B, and C) and catechins that are thought to act on the dopaminergic system — the same neurotransmitter pathway associated with pleasure, motivation, and sexual reward.

Catuaba's circulatory benefits work synergistically with Muira Puama: while Muira Puama supports neurological aspects of libido, Catuaba's effects on vascular tone help ensure that enhanced desire translates into improved physical performance. Together, they create a complementary approach to male sexual wellness from two separate physiological angles.

Ingredient #5: Green Tea Extract (Camellia sinensis)

Primary roles: Clean energy, antioxidant protection, metabolic support

Green tea extract standardized for EGCG (epigallocatechin gallate) is one of the most extensively studied plant compounds in nutritional science. In the context of Soda Peak, it serves two primary functions:

  • Metabolic energy: EGCG has been shown in multiple studies to support fat oxidation and metabolic rate, contributing to sustained physical energy throughout the day.
  • Antioxidant vascular protection: EGCG is a potent antioxidant that specifically protects the endothelium (blood vessel lining) from oxidative stress — the kind of cellular damage that, over time, impairs circulation. Healthy blood vessels are foundational to male performance.

Green tea's naturally occurring L-theanine also modulates the stimulant effect of caffeine, producing what researchers describe as "focused alertness" rather than the jitteriness associated with caffeine alone.

Ingredient #6: Natural Caffeine

Primary roles: Immediate energy, focus, physical performance

Caffeine is the world's most studied ergogenic (performance-enhancing) compound. In Soda Peak's formula, it provides the fast-acting energy and focus component while the other ingredients build slower, more sustained benefits.

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that caffeine enhances physical performance at moderate doses, improves alertness, and reduces perceived exertion — making it a well-validated addition to any male performance formula.

The key advantage of Soda Peak's approach is that natural caffeine from plant sources — rather than synthetic caffeine anhydrous — pairs naturally with the antioxidants and polyphenols in green tea extract, creating a more balanced and sustained energy effect.
